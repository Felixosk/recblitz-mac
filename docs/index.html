How it works

From talking to a note in four steps

Dictation apps paste text into the window you are in. Screen recorders give you a video. RecBlitz gives you something you can find next week.

  1. Click or press ⌥⌘RA voice note starts right away. For a screencast pick Screen or Region first, the microphone always runs along.
  2. Transcribed on the MacApple's SpeechAnalyzer turns 25 seconds of speech into text in about half a second.
  3. A note in your folderMarkdown with a one-line title, an optional summary with next steps and the transcript. Your Obsidian vault works fine.
  4. Optional: Google DriveWith rclone the note also becomes a Google Doc, the video lands next to it and the share link is on your clipboard.
The panel

Everything in one small window

Language, what to capture, the record button and your latest recording with its actions. Same look as its sister app MeetingBlitz.

  • Webcam bubble that is recorded into the video
  • Countdown, pause, restart and discard in a control bar that never shows up in the recording
  • Upload progress in percent
  • German and English interface

Install

Two ways to get it

Quickest

Download the app

Unzip, move RecBlitz.app to Applications, then clear the download quarantine once:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/RecBlitz.app
Why the command? The app is not signed with a paid Apple developer account. The command tells macOS you vouch for it yourself. Only do that for software you trust, or build it yourself.
No warning at all

Build it yourself

Needs the Xcode Command Line Tools (xcode-select --install), about two minutes:

git clone https://github.com/Felixosk/recblitz-mac.git
cd recblitz-mac
./build.sh && open dist/RecBlitz.app

No window, no dock icon: RecBlitz lives in the menu bar as a small waveform. Google Drive, summaries and automatic language detection are optional, the README lists what each helper adds.
