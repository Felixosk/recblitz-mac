New in 3.0: the whole app in Liquid Glass

Say it once. Keep it forever.

Click the icon, talk, click again. RecBlitz transcribes on your Mac and saves a titled note you can search next week.

Download for Mac Source on GitHub

Free for personal use · macOS 14 or newer (transcription needs macOS 26) · Apple Silicon and Intel

Four things it does for you

It lives in the menu bar. No Dock icon, no account, no server.

1 · The panel

One click, or ⌥⌘R

Click the waveform in the menu bar. Pick the language, pick Audio only, Screen or Region, press Start. Or skip the panel: ⌥⌘R starts and stops from anywhere.

Mic and System are two switches, both on by default, so an online call is transcribed with both sides and no video is kept.

2 · The transcript

It is text in half a second

Apple's speech recognition runs on your Mac. A 25 second note takes about half a second. German, English, Spanish or automatic detection.

Nothing leaves the machine for that step.

3 · The note

A titled Markdown note, every time

Title, an optional summary with next steps, and the full transcript in one file. Point the folder at your Obsidian vault and every thought you spoke becomes searchable.

Or switch on Google Drive: the same note becomes a Google Doc, a screencast is uploaded next to it and the link is on your clipboard.

4 · The banner

You see when it is done

A glass capsule rises in when the note or video is ready. One click opens the result. If something fails, it says what.

It is drawn by the app itself, so Do Not Disturb does not hide it.

Light or dark, your call

It follows your Mac, or you pick Light or Dark yourself. The glass can be set clearer for busy wallpapers.

Stays on your Mac

Transcription is on-device. No server, no account, no tracking. Google Drive is off until you switch it on.

Native and small

Swift and SwiftUI, no Electron. The app is 1.8 MB. Read every line on GitHub and build it yourself in two minutes.

Works without setup

Every recording becomes a Markdown note in a folder you choose. Google Doc, Drive link and summary are optional extras.

Put it in your menu bar

Download, unzip, move to Applications. One command on first launch.
